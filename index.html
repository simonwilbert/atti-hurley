How To Play

Eat every pellet to clear the maze. Power pellets turn ghosts blue for a few seconds so you can chomp them for bonus points.

Move: Arrow keys
Pause: Space
Goal: Clear all pellets
Bonus: Eat frightened ghosts
  • Pellet: 10 points
  • Power pellet: 50 points
  • Ghost: 200 points while frightened
